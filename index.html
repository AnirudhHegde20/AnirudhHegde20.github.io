Business Problem

SaaS Churn & Retention Analytics Pipeline

SaaS teams need to understand where users drop off, which behaviors predict retention, and whether the metrics layer behind dashboards is reliable enough for product decisions.

What I Built

Built an end-to-end analytics engineering pipeline ingesting 500K+ engagement records and Product Hunt API data into a layered PostgreSQL warehouse using dbt Core, with raw, staging, and marts models validated by automated tests in CI.

Key Decisions & Challenges

Used dbt Core to replace one-off analysis logic with reusable models and added 42 dbt and dbt-expectations tests through GitHub Actions to catch data quality issues before they reached reporting.

Outcome

Modeled retention using Kaplan-Meier survival analysis and an XGBoost churn model with 0.71 AUC-ROC, identifying a 70% month-1 churn cliff, 8x longer retention for Power Users, and engagement frequency as the top churn driver.

Product Analytics
Business Problem

E-commerce Conversion Optimization

A product team wants to know whether a new product-page experience should be rolled out based on conversion behavior across view, cart, and purchase stages.

What I Built

Built an end-to-end A/B test analysis for a 2,000-user product-page experiment, combining Chi-Square testing, Fisher's Exact segment tests, 95% confidence intervals, and funnel analysis across view, cart, and purchase stages.

Key Decisions & Challenges

Separated the aggregate test from segment-level diagnostics to avoid overreacting to small segment signals while still flagging risk areas for validation.

Outcome

Found no significant overall lift from Treatment B, with conversion moving from 7.5% to 8.8% and p=0.33. Recommended against full rollout while treating Europe + Tablet underperformance as an exploratory signal due to small segment size and multiple-comparison risk.
