Business Problem

AI SaaS Retention & Competitive Intelligence Platform

B2C AI apps lose most users in their first month. This project asks: what early signals predict whether a user will stick around, and how do we build a metrics layer product teams can actually trust?

What I Built

End-to-end analytics platform ingesting 500K+ Yelp events and Product Hunt API data into a layered PostgreSQL warehouse (raw → staging → marts) using dbt Core. Built Kaplan-Meier survival curves, an XGBoost churn model, and a Streamlit dashboard for cohort and competitive intelligence.

Key Decisions & Challenges

Chose dbt for transformation to enforce modular, testable SQL over ad hoc scripts. Used Yelp reviews as a behavioral proxy for SaaS engagement — review frequency mapped to product usage patterns. Designed 42 automated tests across three quality layers (dbt-expectations, database constraints, CI checks) to catch data issues before they hit the dashboard.

What I Learned

Warehouse layering (raw → staging → marts) isn't just architecture — it's discipline. Immutable raw data saved me multiple times when upstream sources changed. The 8x survival gap between Power Users and New Users was only visible because cohort definitions were consistent across the whole pipeline.

Business Problem

Automated Bilingual Complaint Classification System

Banking complaint routing is slow and manual. With complaints arriving in both English and Hindi, how do you build a system that automatically classifies and routes them to the right team — at scale, with fairness checks?

What I Built

Full MLOps pipeline on GCP for bilingual (English/Hindi) complaint classification. Designed Airflow DAGs for data preprocessing, PII anonymization, and schema validation on 500K+ CFPB complaints stored in BigQuery. Fine-tuned mBERT and XLM-RoBERTa, deployed to Vertex AI with automated CI/CD, data drift detection, and Slack monitoring.

Key Decisions & Challenges

PII anonymization was the hardest part — complaints contained bank account numbers, SSNs, addresses, and demographic info. Built a multi-pattern detection system to redact 15+ PII types while preserving complaint meaning. Used cosine similarity on sentence embeddings for drift detection instead of statistical tests — more robust for text data. Added Fairlearn bias checks across product and department slices to ensure the model wasn't systematically failing certain complaint types.

What I Learned

MLOps is mostly plumbing. The model training took days; the pipeline, monitoring, and deployment took weeks. Real production ML is 20% modeling and 80% engineering. mBERT outperformed XLM-RoBERTa on product classification (F1 0.69 vs 0.68) — likely because mBERT's pretraining distribution better matched the financial domain text.

Business Problem

E-commerce Conversion Optimization

A product team wants to ship a new page design. Overall conversion looks similar between variants — but is it safe to roll out globally, or are there segments where the new design actually hurts?

What I Built

A/B test framework for 2,000 users using Chi-Square and Fisher's Exact tests across region and device segments. Built segment interaction analysis that surfaced a critical failure case the overall numbers completely masked.

Key Decisions & Challenges

The overall Chi-Square test showed p=0.33 — not significant, seemingly safe to ship. But segment-level Fisher's Exact tests revealed Europe + Tablet had conversion drop from 18.4% to 0% (p=0.0151). This is a classic Simpson's Paradox-adjacent problem: aggregate statistics can hide segment-level disasters. Built confidence intervals for all segments to give the product team a clear rollout recommendation rather than a binary ship/no-ship.

What I Learned

Never stop at the aggregate result. The most important finding in this project was invisible until segment analysis — and it would have caused real revenue damage if the design had shipped globally. Statistical significance at the overall level means nothing if a specific segment breaks completely.

Business Problem

Customer Churn Prediction Engine

Reduce revenue leakage by predicting churn risk early and explaining key drivers so retention teams can act with confidence — not just a model score, but actionable insight.

What I Built

Churn prediction system using Random Forest and XGBoost achieving 0.83 AUC on 7K records. Implemented SHAP explainability for feature-level insights and profit-curve thresholding to optimize the decision threshold for retention spend ROI rather than raw accuracy.

Key Decisions & Challenges

Standard accuracy optimization is wrong for churn — the cost of missing a churner is much higher than the cost of a false alarm. Used profit-curve analysis to find the threshold that maximized expected ROI on retention campaigns, not just model performance. SHAP values made the model explainable to non-technical stakeholders who needed to trust the predictions.
