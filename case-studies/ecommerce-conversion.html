E-commerce Conversion Lift via A/B Testing

A decision-driven experiment to quantify the impact of a product page redesign, identify where uplift is real, and recommend rollout strategy without risking revenue.

GitHub Repo Contact
Business goal: ship the redesign only where it improves conversion, and avoid a risky “global rollout” decision.

Problem

A redesign was proposed to improve conversion, but the impact could vary by region/device and might even hurt performance in some segments.

Approach

  • Defined primary metric: conversion rate
  • Validated sample size with power analysis
  • Used Chi-square / Fisher’s exact for significance
  • Segmented results by region + device for rollout strategy

Results (high-level)

  • Identified segments with meaningful uplift
  • Found segments with weak/uncertain impact
  • Recommended selective rollout to maximize upside

Tech Stack

  • Python, Pandas
  • Statistical tests + power analysis
  • Plotly charts for stakeholder readability
