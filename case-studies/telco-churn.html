Telco Churn Early-Warning + Retention Targeting

A churn prediction workflow built to flag churn risk early, explain why customers churn, and support retention decisions.

GitHub Repo Contact
Business goal: reduce churn by prioritizing intervention for customers most likely to leave and most valuable to retain.

Problem

Churn reduces recurring revenue. Teams needed a reliable early-warning signal and clarity on what drives churn.

Approach

  • Data prep + feature engineering
  • Train/validate churn models (e.g., tree ensembles)
  • Explain drivers (why churn happens)
  • Translate insights into retention actions

Results (high-level)

  • Accuracy target achieved (~87%)
  • Top drivers surfaced (contract, tenure, charges)
  • Designed for practical retention targeting

Tech Stack

  • Python, scikit-learn / XGBoost
  • Explainability (SHAP-style insights)
  • Dashboard-friendly outputs
